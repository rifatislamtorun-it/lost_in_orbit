2. MISSIONS AND THE NASA DATA BEHIND THEM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MISSION 1: TOUCHDOWN
  Story: You made it down! The outpost is half-powered. Get inside the Habitat, call Mission Control, then find the key systems.
  NASA data used:
    - A sol (Mars day) is about 24.6 hours.
    - Mars is about 1.5 AU from the Sun — sunlight takes ~13 minutes to arrive.
    - NASA suggests airlocks and suitports to keep Mars dust out of living quarters.
  Sources:
    - NASA Mars Facts: https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
    - NASA – Perseverance Offers Clues to Protect Crews From Mars Dust (Oct 26, 2026): https://www.nasa.gov/missions/mars-2020-perseverance/nasas-perseverance-offers-clues-to-protect-crews-from-mars-dust/
    - NASA – Simulated Mars Crew Marks One Year Inside Habitat (Oct 19, 2026): https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/nasas-simulated-mars-crew-marks-one-year-inside-habitat/

MISSION 2: POWER PROBLEM
  Story: Mission Control sees the solar array's output dropping. Go investigate and choose a fix.
  NASA data used:
    - Dust blocks sunlight, so solar panels make less power.
    - Mars is 1.5 AU from the Sun, so sunlight is weaker than on Earth.
  Sources:
    - NASA Mars Facts: https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/

MISSION 3: WATER SHORTAGE
  Story: Water production is falling. Scan for ice with the rover, fix the extractor, then plan the water supply.
  NASA data used:
    - Water on Mars today is ice just under the surface near the poles, plus salty seasonal flows.
    - The air is too thin for liquid water to last long on the surface.
  Sources:
    - NASA Mars Facts: https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
    - NASA Mars Odyssey – Science: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/odyssey/science/

MISSION 4: OXYGEN EMERGENCY
  Story: The Oxygen Generator is unstable! Restore it before the air runs low.
  NASA data used:
    - MOXIE made oxygen from Mars' CO₂ in 16 runs.
    - It produced 122 g in total — up to 12 g/hour at 98%+ purity.
    - “Living off the land” is called in-situ resource utilization (ISRU).
  Sources:
    - NASA JPL – MOXIE Completes Mars Mission: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-oxygen-generating-experiment-moxie-completes-mars-mission/
    - NASA Mars Facts: https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/

MISSION 5: DUST STORM
  Story: Mission Control has detected rising dust activity near the colony. A big storm is on its way!
  NASA data used:
    - Dust storms can cover much of Mars; the dust can take months to settle.
    - Fine dust (under 2.5 micrometers) can get deep into lungs.
    - Suitports and dual-chamber airlocks could keep dust out of habitats.
  Sources:
    - NASA Mars Facts: https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
    - NASA – Perseverance Offers Clues to Protect Crews From Mars Dust (Oct 26, 2026): https://www.nasa.gov/missions/mars-2020-perseverance/nasas-perseverance-offers-clues-to-protect-crews-from-mars-dust/

MISSION 6: RADIATION ALERT
  Story: Radiation sensors are warning of higher exposure. How will you protect the crew?
  NASA data used:
    - Mars has no global magnetic field today.
    - Its thin atmosphere offers little protection.
    - NASA's Human Research Program studies how to keep crews healthy.
  Sources:
    - NASA Mars Facts: https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
    - NASA Mars Exploration – Science Goals: https://science.nasa.gov/planetary-science/programs/mars-exploration/science-goals/
    - NASA Human Research Program: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/

MISSION 7: FOOD CRISIS
  Story: The greenhouse is producing less food. Power and food are tied together — balance them, then share a meal!
  NASA data used:
    - NASA's CHAPEA crew grew crops and managed limited resources for 378 days.
    - Mars gets less sunlight than Earth, so plants need lights and warmth.
  Sources:
    - NASA Mars Facts: https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
    - NASA – Simulated Mars Crew Marks One Year Inside Habitat (Oct 19, 2026): https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/nasas-simulated-mars-crew-marks-one-year-inside-habitat/

MISSION 8: ROVER SURVEY
  Story: Mission Control wants a survey of the crater. Grab the rover checklist, drive out to the crater, and scan the rocks.
  NASA data used:
    - Perseverance explores Jezero Crater.
    - Its panorama shows Neretva Vallis, a long-dried river channel through the crater rim.
    - Rovers help prepare the way for human explorers.
  Sources:
    - NASA – Perseverance Offers Clues to Protect Crews From Mars Dust (Oct 26, 2026): https://www.nasa.gov/missions/mars-2020-perseverance/nasas-perseverance-offers-clues-to-protect-crews-from-mars-dust/
    - NASA Mars 2020: Perseverance Rover: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-2020-perseverance/
    - NASA Mars Science Laboratory: Curiosity Rover: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/msl-curiosity/

MISSION 9: COLD NIGHT
  Story: The sun is setting and a bitterly cold night is coming. Keep the greenhouse warm, check the antenna, and get some sleep.
  NASA data used:
    - Mars temperatures range from about 20 °C down to about −153 °C.
    - The thin atmosphere lets heat escape easily.
    - Phobos and Deimos — Mars' two small moons — rise in the night sky.
    - CHAPEA communication delays reach 45 minutes each way.
  Sources:
    - NASA Mars Facts: https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
    - NASA – Moons of Mars (Phobos & Deimos): https://science.nasa.gov/mars/moons/
    - NASA – Simulated Mars Crew Marks One Year Inside Habitat (Oct 19, 2026): https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/nasas-simulated-mars-crew-marks-one-year-inside-habitat/

MISSION 10: FINAL OUTPOST CHALLENGE
  Story: Everything is under stress at once! Fix each system, then run a 3-sol survival test at the Habitat Console.
  NASA data used:
    - Power, water, oxygen, food, radiation safety and crew health all depend on each other.
    - Real missions have limited resources — NASA's CHAPEA crew managed them for 378 days.
  Sources:
    - NASA Mars Facts: https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
    - NASA JPL – MOXIE Completes Mars Mission: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-oxygen-generating-experiment-moxie-completes-mars-mission/
    - NASA – Perseverance Offers Clues to Protect Crews From Mars Dust (Oct 26, 2026): https://www.nasa.gov/missions/mars-2020-perseverance/nasas-perseverance-offers-clues-to-protect-crews-from-mars-dust/
    - NASA – Simulated Mars Crew Marks One Year Inside Habitat (Oct 19, 2026): https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/nasas-simulated-mars-crew-marks-one-year-inside-habitat/
    - NASA Mars Exploration – Science Goals: https://science.nasa.gov/planetary-science/programs/mars-exploration/science-goals/

3. QUIZ QUESTIONS AND THEIR SOURCES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[q_sol] About how long is one day on Mars (a "sol")?
   Answer: About 24.6 hours
   Source: NASA Mars Facts — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
[q_distance] How far is Mars from the Sun compared with Earth?
   Answer: About 1.5 times farther
   Source: NASA Mars Facts — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
[q_solar_dust] Dust is covering our solar arrays. What happens to our power?
   Answer: It decreases, because dust blocks sunlight
   Source: NASA Mars Facts — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
[q_ice] Where is water found on Mars today?
   Answer: As ice just under the surface near the poles (and some salty seasonal flows)
   Source: NASA Mars Facts — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
[q_tf_lakes] True or false: the Martian air is thick enough for liquid water to stay on the surface for long.
   Answer: False
   Source: NASA Mars Facts — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
[q_oxy] Why is oxygen production important on Mars?
   Answer: Humans cannot breathe Mars' atmosphere.
   Source: NASA Mars Facts — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
[q_moxie] What did NASA's MOXIE experiment on the Perseverance rover do?
   Answer: Made oxygen from Mars' carbon dioxide
   Source: NASA JPL – MOXIE Completes Mars Mission — https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-oxygen-generating-experiment-moxie-completes-mars-mission/
[q_moxie2] How much oxygen did MOXIE make in total?
   Answer: 122 grams — about what a small dog breathes in 10 hours
   Source: NASA JPL – MOXIE Completes Mars Mission — https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-oxygen-generating-experiment-moxie-completes-mars-mission/
[q_storm] Which statement about Martian dust storms is true?
   Answer: Some cover much of the planet, and the dust can take months to settle
   Source: NASA Mars Facts — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
[q_best_storm] A big dust storm is coming. Which plan is BEST for keeping the lights on?
   Answer: Save power: use backup batteries and turn off extra systems
   Source: NASA Mars Facts — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
[q_dust_fine] Perseverance measured Mars dust smaller than 2.5 micrometers. Why does that matter for crews?
   Answer: Tiny dust can get deep into lungs, so habitats must keep dust out
   Source: NASA – Perseverance Offers Clues to Protect Crews From Mars Dust (Oct 26, 2026) — https://www.nasa.gov/missions/mars-2020-perseverance/nasas-perseverance-offers-clues-to-protect-crews-from-mars-dust/
[q_airlock] Which design ideas does NASA mention to reduce dust getting into living quarters?
   Answer: Suitports and dual-chamber airlocks
   Source: NASA – Perseverance Offers Clues to Protect Crews From Mars Dust (Oct 26, 2026) — https://www.nasa.gov/missions/mars-2020-perseverance/nasas-perseverance-offers-clues-to-protect-crews-from-mars-dust/
[q_magnet] Mars has no global magnetic field today. What does that mean for explorers?
   Answer: Radiation protection matters — shelters and shielding help
   Source: NASA Mars Facts — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
[q_tf_shield] True or false: putting lots of material (like soil, rock or water) around a habitat can help shield astronauts from radiation.
   Answer: True
   Source: NASA Mars Exploration – Science Goals — https://science.nasa.gov/planetary-science/programs/mars-exploration/science-goals/
[q_sun] Why might a Mars greenhouse need extra lights and heating?
   Answer: Mars is farther from the Sun, so sunlight is weaker
   Source: NASA Mars Facts — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
[q_food_crew] During NASA's year-long CHAPEA Mars simulation, what did the crew do?
   Answer: Grew crops and managed limited resources
   Source: NASA – Simulated Mars Crew Marks One Year Inside Habitat (Oct 19, 2026) — https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/nasas-simulated-mars-crew-marks-one-year-inside-habitat/
[q_jezero] Perseverance explores Jezero Crater. What is Neretva Vallis, seen in its panorama?
   Answer: A long-dried river channel cutting through the crater rim
   Source: NASA – Perseverance Offers Clues to Protect Crews From Mars Dust (Oct 26, 2026) — https://www.nasa.gov/missions/mars-2020-perseverance/nasas-perseverance-offers-clues-to-protect-crews-from-mars-dust/
[q_comm_delay] In NASA's CHAPEA Mars analog, messages to Earth were delayed by up to how long each way?
   Answer: Up to 45 minutes
   Source: NASA – Simulated Mars Crew Marks One Year Inside Habitat (Oct 19, 2026) — https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/nasas-simulated-mars-crew-marks-one-year-inside-habitat/
[q_cold] Why can Mars get extremely cold at night?
   Answer: Its thin atmosphere lets heat escape easily
   Source: NASA Mars Facts — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/
[q_moons] What are the names of Mars' two small moons?
   Answer: Phobos and Deimos
   Source: NASA – Moons of Mars (Phobos & Deimos) — https://science.nasa.gov/mars/moons/
